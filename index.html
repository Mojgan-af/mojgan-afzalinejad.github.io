About Me

As a Senior Data Analyst, I transform raw data into meaningful insights that highlight trends, optimize processes, and support strategic decision-making. With extensive experience in Power BI, I develop interactive dashboards that allow stakeholders to easily track and monitor sales and customer KPIs. On the BI side, I translate business requirements into scalable models. I design data models aligned with business needs, leveraging efficient DAX to enhance BI reporting. On the data engineering side, I build and automate ETL workflows using Azure Data Factory and SSIS, ensuring consistent and high-quality data delivery. Additionally, I bring strong knowledge of data warehousing principles, including dimensional modeling, schema design, and the creation of optimized fact and dimension tables to support BI solution.
